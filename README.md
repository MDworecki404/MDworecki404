### 👋 About me

I'm a frontend developer and a surveyor by training. I build websites where **the map is the most important element**: 2D and 3D geoportals that run in the browser, with nothing to install.

- 💼 **Now:** Junior Frontend Developer at [infoSolutions](https://info-solutions.pl/), working on WebGIS software in TypeScript and Vue
- 🎓 **Education:** MSc Eng. in Geodesy, Surveying and Cartography, Wrocław University of Environmental and Life Sciences
- 🌍 **Focus:** CesiumJS, OpenLayers, 3D city models, spatial data (GUGiK, BDOT10k, FME)
- 🌱 **Exploring:** Svelte 5 / SvelteKit, Angular, Docker
- 📍 **Based in:** Wrocław, Poland
